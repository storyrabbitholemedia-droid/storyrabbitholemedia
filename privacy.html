Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 23, 2026

StoryRabbitHoleMedia uses TikTok integrations to support content publishing and performance analysis for accounts that authorize the application.

Information we access

When an authorized TikTok account connects, we may access basic account information and public video information made available through TikTok's APIs, including video performance metrics permitted by granted scopes.

How information is used

Information is used to operate StoryRabbitHoleMedia's content workflow, upload content to an authorized account, and analyze published content performance.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information accessed through the integration is used for StoryRabbitHoleMedia operations and is not shared with third parties except as necessary to operate relevant platform services or as required by law.

Data retention and deletion

Data is retained only as reasonably necessary for the content workflow and performance analysis. An authorized user may revoke TikTok access through TikTok account settings. Locally retained integration data can be deleted when no longer required.

Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect credentials and information used by the application.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the application or its integrations change. The effective date above reflects the latest revision.
