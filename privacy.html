Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how StoryRabbitHoleMedia handles information used by its website and application integrations.

Information We Access

When an authorized TikTok account connects to StoryRabbitHoleMedia, the application may access basic account information and public video information made available through TikTok APIs, including video performance information permitted by the scopes authorized by the account.

How Information Is Used

Information is used to operate the StoryRabbitHoleMedia content workflow, upload content to authorized accounts, retrieve information about published videos, and analyze content performance.

Sharing

StoryRabbitHoleMedia does not sell personal information. Information accessed through an integration is used for StoryRabbitHoleMedia operations and is not shared with third parties except as necessary to operate connected platform services or as required by law.

Data Retention and Access

Integration data is retained only as reasonably necessary to operate the content workflow and analyze performance. Users may revoke TikTok authorization through the controls provided by TikTok. Locally retained integration data may be deleted when it is no longer required.

Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect credentials and information used by the application.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the application or its integrations change. The effective date above reflects the latest revision.
